Nilo Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-11 · Last updated: 2026-11-11

Nilo is dictation that runs on your phone. There is no Nilo account, cloud transcription or advertising. Nilo does not upload your speech or text. Model-download providers and Google Play may receive technical request, identifier and diagnostic data as described below. Text you insert into another app or explicitly export is handled by that destination.

1. Who we are

Nilo is developed and published by KindaSoren ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the Nilo Android application (the "app") handles your information. It applies to the Android app. For any privacy question or request, email support@sorens.dev.

2. How dictation works, and what happens to your voice

Nilo transcribes speech on your device using the Whisper model and the whisper.cpp engine. The engine is bundled with the app; speech models are downloaded or imported separately. The microphone is open only while you are dictating or while a Listen to speaker audio session you started is running. Audio is held in memory for the current session and then discarded.

  • Audio is never transmitted to us or to any third party.
  • Audio is never written to storage by the app.
  • Android shows an ongoing notification while the microphone is active, and you can stop dictation at any time.

Listen to speaker audio

“Listen to speaker audio” transcribes a message that another app, such as WhatsApp, plays through your phone's speaker. You start it from Nilo's Home screen or from Nilo's notification. Nilo records what the microphone hears, so it captures the voice of whoever is speaking in that message as well as any nearby sound. It does not read other apps' audio internally, and it does not work with headphones.

  • A session lasts at most two minutes and ends when you tap Done or cancel it.
  • The audio is handled exactly like dictation: transcribed on your device, kept only in memory, never written to storage and never uploaded.
  • The transcript is not typed into any app or copied to the clipboard until you choose Copy or Paste. If history is on, it is saved to your local history like a dictation.
  • Only transcribe audio you have the right to transcribe. The person speaking in the message is not notified by Nilo.

Voice messages you share to Nilo

You can also share a voice message or audio file to Nilo from another app, for example by long-pressing a voice note in WhatsApp and choosing Share, then Nilo. Nilo reads that one file through the permission Android grants for the share, decodes it in memory, transcribes it on your device and then discards the audio. Nilo does not copy or save the file, does not upload it, and cannot read other files. The transcript is handled as described above: it waits for you to copy, share or paste it, and is saved to your local history only if history is on.

3. The Accessibility service

Nilo offers an optional Android Accessibility service that types transcribed text into the field you are already using, so you do not have to switch keyboards. If you enable it, the service:

  • Reads the currently focused editable field and basic window state, only while dictation is enabled;
  • Uses that information solely to insert the text you dictated;
  • Ignores password fields and never reads or stores their contents;
  • Never logs, saves to disk or uploads existing field contents. Previous field text may be held temporarily in memory to support Undo;
  • Never clicks buttons or changes settings; it only inserts or restores text when you request it.

You can turn the service off at any time in Android Settings → Accessibility. If you decline it, Nilo's other insertion methods (the Nilo keyboard and the clipboard) remain available.

4. The Nilo keyboard (optional)

Nilo includes an optional Android keyboard (input method). If you enable it and select it as your keyboard, it composes the text you dictate. It does not log keystrokes or upload what you dictate. Dictated text follows the history settings described below, and the keyboard contains no advertising or tracking code. You can switch back to another keyboard at any time.

5. Data stored on your device

Transcript history (optional)

If history is on, the text of your dictations is stored in the app's private storage so you can review, search and export it. History can be turned off in Settings, individual entries can be deleted, and "Clear history" removes all of it. It is never synced and never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it.

Usage statistics

Nilo keeps counts on your device, such as words dictated, speaking pace and day streak. These are numbers only, stored locally, and are not linked to your identity.

Dictionary, snippets, presets and preferences

Everything you add or configure is stored locally. You can export dictionary entries to the clipboard and transcript history to a file location you choose.

6. Network access and model downloads

Models are downloaded over HTTPS from Hugging Face and its download hosts. These providers receive IP addresses, request times, requested model paths and technical headers. Hugging Face documents approximate country/region information and hashed IP addresses in download analytics, and uses IP addresses for rate limiting. This metadata supports delivery, analytics and platform security under the Hugging Face privacy policy. Model requests contain no audio, transcripts, dictionary, snippets or local dictation statistics. You can avoid this download flow by importing a model file from your device.

Nilo also communicates with Google Play for prices, purchases and ownership checks on startup and resume, even when the Pro purchase UI is disabled. The Play Billing library sends diagnostic events and client-session identifiers to Google, with information such as response/error codes, SDK/app version and device/network context. These events support diagnostics and service improvement under Google's privacy policy. The current app has no switch to disable Billing's diagnostic path. These transfers use encrypted connections and do not include your dictated audio or text.

Feedback you send. “Send feedback” in Settings and “Report” on a transcript open an email to support@sorens.dev in your own email app; Nilo does not send anything itself. The draft contains the app version, speech model, language, insertion method, Android version and device model, and for a report the text of that transcript and the app it went to. You can read, edit or delete any of it, or not send it at all. If you send it, we receive your email address and what you wrote, use them only to reply and to fix problems, and delete them on request.

7. Purchases

If you buy a paid feature such as Nilo Pro, the purchase is processed by Google Play under Google's privacy policy. We receive only a confirmation that a purchase exists, its product identifier and order information needed to grant and restore the feature. We never receive your name, card number, billing address or other payment details. Purchases are tied to your Google account, which is how they can be restored after reinstalling the app or moving to a new phone.

8. What Nilo never does

  • No Nilo account creation or sign-in.
  • No collection of personal data on our servers — we do not operate a service that receives it.
  • No advertising SDKs or tracking pixels. Provider download analytics and Billing diagnostics are described above.
  • No selling or renting of personal data. Technical data is transferred to the providers described in section 6; user-requested insertion, export and feedback emails transfer text to the destination you choose.
  • No advertising.
  • No microphone use while dictation is off.

10. Security

Local data is kept in the app's private storage, protected by Android's app sandbox. Network traffic uses HTTPS. Because we do not run a backend that stores your data, there is no server-side copy of your speech, transcripts or dictionary to breach or to request.

11. Retention and deletion

Transcript history retains at most the latest 500 entries. Older entries are removed as new ones arrive. Other saved data remains on your device until you delete it. You can delete individual history entries, clear all history, disable history, remove dictionary entries and snippets, or uninstall the app, which removes all app data from the device. Nilo opts out of Android cloud backup, so none of this data is copied to Google Drive. If you move to a new phone with a direct phone-to-phone transfer, Android may copy it to the new phone. Because there is no account and no server-side storage, there is nothing further for us to delete. If you believe we hold data about you and want it removed, email support@sorens.dev.

Clearing local data does not delete provider-held download logs or Billing diagnostics. Those providers set their retention periods and handle applicable access/deletion requests under their own policies; we do not control or promise deletion of their records. For Hugging Face privacy requests, contact privacy@huggingface.co. Google provides privacy controls.

12. Children

Nilo is not directed to children under 13. Audio and transcripts are processed locally for all users; technical provider data handling is described in section 6.

13. Third-party components

  • OpenAI Whisper and whisper.cpp are MIT-licensed components executed locally on your device.
  • Google Play handles purchases and ownership checks; its Billing library also sends diagnostics and session identifiers.
  • Hugging Face and its download hosts serve models and process technical request metadata.
